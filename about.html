About

GoalX was built by
Chad Burmeister.

It exists because a question kept coming up in sales conversations that had nothing to do with sales: most people cannot say clearly what they actually want.

Who

Twenty-five years of asking people what they're really after

Chad Burmeister is a sales and business development leader based in Denver, Colorado. He has spent more than two decades building and running sales organizations — at WebEx, ON24, RingCentral, Riverbed, ConnectAndSell, and most recently Informatica, a Salesforce company.

He hosts The AI for Sales Podcast, publishing twice weekly, and has written nine books across two subjects that turn out to be the same subject: how people decide what to pursue, and what happens when they get clear about it. He holds an MBA in Computer Science from Loyola Marymount University, is a Forbes Next 1000 honoree, and has been named a Top Inside Sales Leader for ten consecutive years.

GoalX is not a sales tool. It came out of noticing, across thousands of conversations, that the people who moved fastest were not the ones with the best tactics — they were the ones who could say precisely what they were moving toward.

Why Skip

The guide is named after a real person

Skip Miller mentored Chad at three different companies. Somewhere along the way he stopped being a mentor and became a friend.

He founded M3 Learning in 1995 — the day before he turned forty — wrote seven books, and taught ProActive Selling to more than 300,000 people across 35 countries. He had a way of asking the question you'd been avoiding, and then waiting.

William “Skip” Richard Miller · November 3, 1955 — December 28, 2023

The guide in this app carries his name because of the way he asked questions. It is not him, and it does not speak for him — no software could. It's a thank-you.
